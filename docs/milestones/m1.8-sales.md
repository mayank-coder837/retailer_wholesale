# M1.8 — Sales (plan)

Goal: the selling side of a trader's day — a quotation the customer accepts, the order, goods delivered (in part or
in full, the delivery note without prices), the invoice (cash or on credit, cost of goods sold moved from "goods
delivered not invoiced"), returns, and the controls around it: the price suggested first (the last price sold to
this customer), the minimum-price guard, the credit check, round-off on the bill total, and Cancel / Reverse with a
warning when the document was already sent.

Roadmap acceptance: the full flow posts per rules 1 to 6; ageing is correct; a cancelled invoice disappears from
every report and its number is reissued. Proving tests: posting tests per rule; fulfilment-quantity tests; ageing
buckets; cancel / reverse tests including allocations becoming advances and delivery notes returning to "not
invoiced".

It reuses what is built: the pricing engine (M1.3: step 1 "last price to this customer" waits for this milestone's
data), the credit check and its audited override (M1.4: "open orders" waits for this milestone), bills and
bill-wise settlement and the receipt voucher (M1.5, rule 6), the stock ledger (M1.6), the document model, open-line
matching and the screens' patterns from purchasing (M1.7), and Cancel / Reverse for every document type (M1.7b).

Three pull requests:

- **M1.8a** — this plan; the six sales documents, their posting (rules 1 to 4 and round-off), fulfilment, the
  credit check and the minimum-price guard at post, the last-sale source and the customer history; the API.
- **M1.8b** — receivables and payables ageing; "sent" and the reuse warning; the sales cases of Cancel / Reverse;
  a property test over random sales flows.
- **M1.8c** — screens.

## Documents (ADR 0009: `document` + `document_line`)

Prices are per unit entered and VAT-exclusive; VAT per line at 0 or 5 % (A3). Price modes (VAT-inclusive, no VAT
with a reason) and tax codes arrive with M1.10, as for purchasing.

| doc_type | Lines | Posts (rule) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quotation` | item, quantity in a unit, price, VAT rate; `valid_until` | nothing. **Numbered when saved** (domain model: "at save for quotations"): it is the document the customer sees, so there is no draft; a change is a new quotation (the old one cancelled) |
| `proforma_invoice` | as a quotation | nothing: a priced document for advance payment. Numbered at post. A receipt can take the advance against its number (the advance's reference) |
| `sales_order` | item, quantity, price, VAT rate; the quotation line it follows (optional) | nothing: an order. Posting confirms it (credit check) and numbers it. Its open quantity counts in "open orders" for the credit check and as **ordered, not delivered** beside stock (shown, not blocking: no hard reservation in M1.8) |
| `delivery_note` | item, quantity delivered, the order line it fulfils (optional) — **no prices** | stock out at the current average; Dr `goods_delivered_not_invoiced` / Cr `inventory` (2) |
| `sales_invoice` | the delivery line it bills, or the order line, or neither (a direct sale); item, quantity, price, VAT rate; optional `cash_account_id` (a cash sale) | credit: Dr `ar_control` (customer) / Cr `sales`, Cr `vat_output`, and the cost — after a delivery note: Dr `cogs` / Cr `goods_delivered_not_invoiced` (the delivery's cost for the billed quantity, 3); without one: stock out, Dr `cogs` / Cr `inventory` (1). Cash sale: Dr the cash or bank account instead of `ar_control`, no bill (1) |
| `sales_return` | the invoice line it returns, quantity | stock back **at the cost it left at**: Dr `inventory` / Cr `cogs`; Dr `sales_returns`, Dr `vat_output` / Cr `ar_control` at the invoice's price and VAT (4). It settles the invoice's bill first; anything over is the customer's advance |

- **Fulfilment (never a stored counter):** an order line's delivered quantity is the sum of posted delivery lines
  that follow it, plus invoice lines that follow it directly; a delivery line's invoiced quantity is the sum of
  posted invoice lines that follow it; an invoice line's returned quantity the sum of posted return lines. No line
  may take more than is still open on the line it follows. Orders are `open`, `partly delivered`, `delivered`
  (or invoiced); delivery notes are `not invoiced`, `partly invoiced`, `invoiced`. A cancelled or reversed document
  stops counting, so a delivery note whose invoice is cancelled is "not invoiced" again (A8, roadmap).
- **Prices on the invoice:** a line following an order (directly or through a delivery note) defaults to the
  order's price; otherwise the price comes from the pricing engine (`POST /pricing/quote`, as typed in the screen).
  The line keeps `price_source` (e.g. `last_price:INV-2026-00412`, `price_list:Wholesale`).
- **Cost moved by an invoice after delivery (A8):** the delivery line's value × billed quantity / delivered
  quantity; the last invoice takes what is left, so `goods_delivered_not_invoiced` clears to exactly zero per
  delivery line (as GRNI per receipt line, M1.7).
- **Cost on a return:** the invoice line's cost per unit (the COGS it moved) × returned quantity, the last return
  taking what is left; goods come back at that value (stock ledger: inbound at a stated value; rule 27 if below
  zero).
- **Receivable:** a posted credit invoice creates a receivable `bill` for its grand total, reference = the invoice
  number, due after the customer's credit days. Receipts settle it bill-wise as today (rule 6).
- **Round-off (L4, rule 34):** when `rounding.invoice_total` is on, the grand total is rounded to its step and the
  difference posts to `rounding`; the bill and the receipt use the rounded total. Off by default.
- **Minimum price (M1.3):** a line below the unit's minimum price is refused at post unless the user holds
  `price.override_min` (then the line keeps the flag; audited with the post).
- **Credit check (M1.4) at order and credit-invoice post:** exposure = ledger AR balance + open orders + this
  document. `block` refuses unless the user holds `credit.override` and gives a reason (recorded with
  `record_credit_override`); `warn` posts and returns the warning. Cash sales are not checked. Open orders =
  the open value of the customer's posted orders (open quantity × price, with VAT).
- **Last price and history (A9):** the last-sale source the pricing engine expects reads posted sales invoices
  (latest first; not cancelled or reversed); `GET /parties/{id}/sales-history?business_id&q` lists every item
  invoiced to the customer: date, invoice number, item, quantity, unit, price — newest first, searchable.
- Gap-free numbers per branch (`QT-`, `PI-`, `SO-`, `DN-`, `INV-`, `SR-`), period lock and override, drafts
  (except quotations), save-and-post, `reuse_number` — as every document so far.

## Model changes (migration 0013)

`document`: `valid_until` (quotations, proformas); a cash sale keeps its cash or bank account in the existing
`account_id` (as a voucher does; the API calls it `cash_account_id`). `document_line`: `price_source`, `below_min`
(the line was let through below the minimum price). Everything else exists (`unit_price`, `net_amount`,
`vat_rate`, `vat_amount`, `line_total`, `source_line_id`, `cleared_value` = the cost a delivery or invoice line
moved, or a return line brought back). A delivery line's cost still in GDNI is read from the stock ledger (its
moves, recosts included), so a later recost never leaves GDNI unexplained.

Built in M1.8a as planned. One addition: the credit check of an invoice that follows an order counts only the
part not already in "open orders", so the customer is never counted twice.

## Ageing and "sent" (M1.8b) — built

- `GET /receivables/ageing?business_id` and `GET /payables/ageing?business_id` (`report.view`): per party, open
  bills by days past due (due date, else bill date): not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90; advances not yet
  allocated in their own column; total = buckets − advances; totals. Read from `bill` (the cache of allocations),
  so it agrees with the party ledgers. **Today's figures** (business time zone): ageing on a past date needs the
  allocations re-dated and comes with the reports (M1.11). M1.8c shows it on screen.
- `POST /documents/{id}/sent` (`<type>.export`) marks a posted document printed, emailed or sent on WhatsApp
  (`sent_at`, the first time; audited as an update). M1.10's printing and later the WhatsApp send call it.
  `GET /documents/{id}` and the cancel answer carry `sent_at`; `GET /documents/released-numbers` gives `was_sent`
  for each freed number, so the screen warns before reusing it (posting-rules, Cancel and Reverse). A sent number
  can still be reused after the warning.
- Fixed on the way (M1.7b): a number freed, reused and freed again (the reissue cancelled too) failed on a unique
  key; it now opens its one released-number row again.

## Cancel and Reverse — the sales cases (M1.7b's one service)

- Sales invoice → reversed by a **credit note** (already the mapping); cancel frees `INV-…` for reissue.
- Delivery note followed by an invoice: refused until the invoice is undone; after that it is "not invoiced" again
  and can itself be cancelled (stock back at the cost it left at, GDNI cleared).
- Receipts allocated to a cancelled invoice become advances (M1.7b); tests prove it for sales.
- Quotation and sales order are cancel-only (nothing in the books); proforma likewise.

## API

Permissions exist (M0.3): `quotation.*`, `sales_order.*`, `delivery_note.*`, `sales_invoice.*`,
`sales_return.*`, `price.override_min`, `credit.override`. M1.8a adds `proforma_invoice.*`; the migration gives each
role the proforma permissions matching what it holds on `sales_invoice` (so the Salesperson and Cashier keep their
reach).

- `GET/POST /sales-documents`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /sales-documents/{id}`, `POST /sales-documents/{id}/post` (body:
  override reasons for the period lock and the credit block, `reuse_number`)
- `GET /sales-documents/{id}/open-lines` — what is still to deliver or invoice (an order), to invoice (a delivery
  note), to return (an invoice)
- `GET /parties/{id}/sales-history`
- M1.8b: `GET /receivables/ageing`, `GET /payables/ageing`, `POST /documents/{id}/sent`

## Tests

- **Posting (pure):** rules 1 (credit and cash), 2, 3 (GDNI share, last takes the rest), 4 (cost it left at), and
  round-off on the total.
- **API:** quotation → order → two partial deliveries → invoice of the first → GDNI zero per delivered line;
  direct invoice moves stock and COGS; over-delivering and over-invoicing refused; cash sale makes no bill; return
  against an invoice reduces its bill, over-return refused; prices default from the order and last price shows on
  the next quote; minimum price refused without the permission; credit block refused, override recorded; warn
  passes with the warning; open orders count in the credit figures; history lists only this customer's items;
  permissions; numbering (quotation numbered at save); period lock.
- **M1.8b:** ageing buckets on fixed dates; advances column; cancel an invoice → bill closed, receipt becomes an
  advance, delivery note "not invoiced", number reissued; reverse → credit note; sent marker and the reuse
  warning.
- **Property (Hypothesis, database):** random orders, partial deliveries, invoices (direct and after delivery, cash
  and credit) and returns, with random cancels and reversals → the trial balance balances, GDNI = Σ (delivered −
  invoiced) cost, stock value = the inventory account, each customer's balance = their open bills.

## Screens (M1.8c) — built

- **Sales** in the sidebar: the list (type and status filters, customer names), "New quotation", "New order",
  "Deliver goods", "New invoice", "Proforma". A return starts from its invoice.
- **Entry form** for all six types; `?from=<doc>` starts from an earlier one with the customer, warehouse and open
  lines. Each unpriced line shows the **pricing engine's suggestion** (last price to this customer first, with where
  it came from); a typed price is the user's own (`price_source: manual`). Below the minimum price the line warns.
  A delivery note has no price fields. An invoice can be a **cash sale** (paid into a cash or bank account). A
  quotation is saved straight away (numbered). Freed numbers can be reused, with a warning when the cancelled
  document had been sent.
- **Customer history panel** under the form: what this customer bought, newest first, searchable; "Add" puts the
  item on the document at its last price.
- **Credit block** on post: the figures and the reasons; someone with `credit.override` gives a reason and posts
  anyway; a warning (policy warn, or the override) shows on the document page after posting.
- **Document page**: header, lines (a delivery note: quantities only), totals with round-off, progress, the next
  step, **Mark as sent**, and Cancel / Reverse (cancelling a sent document warns first).
- **Ageing**: Accounts → Ageing (customers owe / we owe suppliers, today's figures), and this customer's row on
  their page.

# M1.5 — Accounting core (plan)

Goal: the general ledger every later document posts into — a chart of accounts with account roles, fiscal years
with monthly periods and a lock, an immutable double-entry journal, customer and supplier bills settled
bill-wise (like Tally), and the vouchers an accountant types every day: receipt, payment, contra, journal,
expense, credit note and debit note.

Roadmap acceptance: an unbalanced entry is impossible; a locked period rejects posting without the override.
Proving tests: DB trigger test (an unbalanced commit fails), Hypothesis (random balanced vouchers keep the trial
balance at zero), immutability tests.

Two pull requests, as before: **M1.5a** (this plan, model, posting, API, tests) and **M1.5b** (screens).

## Chart of accounts (decision A11)

- One chart per business, seeded from `apps/api/seeds/chart_of_accounts/uae_trading.json` (generic UAE
  trading chart, English and Arabic). It is installed when a business is created (sign-up and
  `POST /businesses`); migration 0009 installs it for businesses that already exist.
- `account`: `business_id`, `code` (unique per business), `name_en/_ar`, `parent_id`, `is_group`, `nature`
  (`asset, liability, equity, income, expense`), `account_type` (ledgers only: `cash, bank, receivable,
  payable, stock, tax, current_asset, fixed_asset, current_liability, non_current_liability, equity, sales,
  other_income, cogs, expense`), `allow_manual_posting`, `is_system` (seeded), `archived_at`.
  A child has its parent's nature; only ledgers (not groups) take postings; a ledger with postings keeps its code
  and type; an account with postings or a role is archived, never deleted.
- `account_role`: `business_id`, `role`, `account_id` — the roles of `domain-model.md` → Accounting. Posting
  code asks for a role, never a code. Changing a role's account affects only later postings (audited).
- **Receivable and payable accounts are control accounts**: every line on them names a customer or supplier
  (the party ledger is those lines); no other line may name a party.
- The account with the `inventory` role is never posted by hand (posting rule 14): `allow_manual_posting` is
  false and cannot be turned on. Stock documents (M1.6) post to it.
- Cash and bank are account types, not roles: a business may have several of each.

## Fiscal years and periods

- `fiscal_year`: `business_id`, `code` (`2026`, or `2026-27` when the year starts after January; also the
  numbering series key, so numbers read `RV-2026-00001`), `start_date`, `end_date`, `status` (`open`;
  `closed` with year-end in Phase 2).
- `fiscal_period`: `fiscal_year_id`, `period_no` (1–12), `start_date`, `end_date`, `status` (`open, locked`),
  `locked_at`, `locked_by`. Months follow `business.fiscal_year_start_month`.
- **Default (configurable later):** posting on a date with no fiscal year creates it automatically when the
  date falls in the current or the next fiscal year (so 1 January never stops the counter); an earlier or
  later year is created on purpose with `POST /fiscal-years`.
- `PUT /fiscal-periods/{id}/lock` and `/unlock` need `fiscal_period.edit` (Accountant, Owner); both audited.
- Posting into a locked period needs `period.override_lock` and a reason; the override is audited (action
  `override`, `changes.override = "period_lock"`). Without it: `409 period_locked`.

## Journal (CLAUDE.md rules 3 and 4)

- `journal_entry`: `business_id`, `branch_id`, `entry_date`, `fiscal_period_id`, `number` (the source
  document's number), `narration`, `source_document_id`, `reversal_of_id`, `posted_at`.
- `journal_line`: `entry_id`, `business_id`, `entry_date` (copied, tied to the entry by a composite key, for
  ledger queries), `line_no`, `account_id`, `party_id`, `debit`, `credit` (base currency, `NUMERIC(19,4)`,
  `≥ 0`, exactly one `> 0`), `description`.
- **Balanced:** a deferred constraint trigger checks Σ debit = Σ credit per entry at commit, and that every
  entry has lines. The service checks the same before it writes, so users see a clear error.
- **Immutable:** the app role has no `UPDATE`/`DELETE` on either table, and a trigger refuses even the owner.
- Base currency only; foreign-currency columns arrive with multi-currency (Phase 2). Cost centres: Phase 2.
- Posting code (`app/modules/accounting/posting.py`) is pure: document → list of `(role or account, party,
  debit, credit)`. One function per voucher type, tested against `posting-rules.md`.

## Bills and bill-wise settlement

- `bill`: `business_id`, `party_id`, `account_id`, `reference` (invoice number or the name the user gives),
  `bill_date`, `due_date`, `direction` (`debit`: the party owes us / we paid in advance; `credit`: we owe them /
  they paid in advance), `amount`, `amount_open` (cached, `0 ≤ open ≤ amount`), `is_advance`,
  `document_id`, `journal_line_id`. Only `amount_open` ever changes (trigger).
- `bill_allocation` (immutable): `bill_id`, `journal_line_id`, `amount > 0`.
- Every party line of a voucher is allocated in full, as in Tally:

| Type | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `against_ref` | Settles an open bill of the same party and account on the opposite side (amount ≤ open) |
| `new_ref` | Creates a bill in the line's direction (e.g. a journal charging a customer; opening bills) |
| `advance` | Creates an advance bill (a receipt before the invoice); invoices settle it later |
| `on_account` | Not tied to a bill; shown separately in ageing |

- Sales invoices (M1.8) and purchase bills (M1.7) create their bills with the same service.
- The credit check (M1.4) now gets the **ledger balance** and the **age of the oldest open receivable bill**
  from accounting. Open orders (M1.8) and PDCs (M1.9) stay zero.

## Vouchers (ADR 0009: `document` + voucher lines)

- `document` (documents module, shared with sales and purchasing later): `doc_type`, `business_id`,
  `branch_id`, `series_id`, `number` (null until posted), `doc_date`, `reference`, `narration`, `party_id`,
  `account_id` (the voucher's cash/bank account), `currency`, `total`, `status` (`draft, posted, cancelled,
  reversed`), `posted_at/by`, `cancelled_at/by`, `cancel_reason`, `reversal_of_id`, `reissue_of_id`,
  `sent_at`. A posted document is immutable except the status change to `cancelled`/`reversed` and its link
  columns (trigger). Sales and purchase columns are added by their milestones.
- `voucher_line`: `document_id`, `line_no`, `account_id`, `party_id`, `debit`, `credit`, `vat_rate`,
  `vat_amount`, `description`. `voucher_allocation`: the bill-wise choices of a draft (line or header,
  type, bill, new reference name, due date, amount); at post they become bills and `bill_allocation` rows.
- How each type is entered (Tally's single-entry mode, except journal and contra) and posted:

| Type | Entered as | Posts (rule) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| receipt | cash/bank account + lines (party or account, amount) | Dr cash/bank / Cr each line (6) |
| payment | cash/bank account + lines | Dr each line / Cr cash/bank (11) |
| expense | cash/bank account + expense lines with a VAT rate | Dr expense, Dr `vat_input` / Cr cash/bank (12) |
| contra | debit and credit lines, cash and bank accounts only | as entered (13) |
| journal | debit and credit lines, any ledger allowed by hand | as entered (14) |
| credit_note | customer + lines (account, default `sales_returns`) with a VAT rate | Dr lines, Dr `vat_output` / Cr customer (5) |
| debit_note | supplier + lines (account, default `purchase_price_variance`) with a VAT rate | Dr supplier / Cr lines, Cr `vat_input` (10) |

- **VAT on vouchers (until tax codes, M1.10):** a line's `vat_rate` is 0% or 5%, VAT-exclusive; VAT per line
  is rounded half-up to fils and the total VAT is the sum of the lines (A3). Tax codes replace the rate in
  M1.10 (5% → `SR`, 0% → `ZR`), which also gives credit and debit notes their VAT 201 boxes.
- **Round-off (L4, rule 34):** when `rounding.receipt.*` / `rounding.payment.*` is on, the cash/bank amount
  of a receipt / payment is rounded and the difference posts to the `rounding` role. Off by default.
- Numbers come from the branch series at post (`RV-2026-00001`, `PV-…`, `CV-…`, `JV-…`, `EV-…`, `CN-…`,
  `DBN-…`): gap-free, and a failed post returns the number (A12).
- Cancel and Reverse for vouchers come with sales (M1.8), which builds them for every document type.

## API

Permissions already exist (M0.3): `account.*`, `<voucher type>.*` (branch-scoped), `fiscal_period.view/edit`,
`period.override_lock`, `report.view`.

- `GET /accounts?business_id=` (tree order, with balances), `POST /accounts`, `PATCH /accounts/{id}`
- `GET /account-roles?business_id=`, `PUT /account-roles/{role}` `{business_id, account_id}`
- `GET /fiscal-years?business_id=` (with periods), `POST /fiscal-years`, `PUT /fiscal-periods/{id}/lock`,
  `PUT /fiscal-periods/{id}/unlock`
- `GET /vouchers?business_id=&doc_type=&status=&party_id=&from=&to=`, `POST /vouchers` (draft; `?post=true`
  saves and posts in one step), `GET/PUT/DELETE /vouchers/{id}` (drafts only), `POST /vouchers/{id}/post`
  `{override_lock_reason?}`
- `GET /bills?business_id=&party_id=&open=true` (for allocation), `GET /accounts/{id}/ledger?from=&to=`,
  `GET /parties/{id}/ledger?business_id=&from=&to=` (statement with running balance),
  `GET /trial-balance?business_id=&as_of=` (a minimal one; full reports in M1.11)

All writes accept `Idempotency-Key` (the middleware already does).

## Tests

- **DB:** an unbalanced entry fails at commit; an entry without lines fails; `journal_line`, `journal_entry`,
  `bill_allocation` and posted documents/lines reject `UPDATE`/`DELETE` (app role and owner); a bill's
  fields other than `amount_open` cannot change; isolation factories for every new table.
- **Property (Hypothesis):** random balanced journals and receipts/payments keep the trial balance at zero and
  each party's balance equal to the sum of its open bills plus on-account amounts; VAT per line adds up to the
  total.
- **Posting:** each voucher type against its rule (5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 34) with the rule's figures.
- **API:** chart installed on sign-up and `POST /businesses`; account create/edit/archive rules; roles;
  periods auto-created, lock/unlock, locked period refused, override with permission and audit; draft → post
  numbering (gap-free, a failed post returns the number); bill-wise (new ref, against ref over/under open,
  advance, on account, wrong party); party required on control accounts only; inventory refused by hand;
  permissions per type and branch; credit check with real balance and overdue age.
- **Screens (M1.5b):** chart of accounts, voucher entry for each type (keyboard-first, bill-wise picker),
  voucher list and view, fiscal periods with lock, account and party ledgers, trial balance; English and
  Arabic.

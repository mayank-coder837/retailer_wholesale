# M1.6 — Inventory (plan)

Goal: the stock ledger every later document moves goods through — immutable stock moves, one moving-average cost
per item across all warehouses (ADR 0006), negative stock allowed with a warning and revalued on arrival, batches
and expiry, and the three inventory documents: stock adjustment, warehouse transfer and Item Transfer.

Roadmap acceptance: stock value = inventory account after any sequence of moves, including negative stock and item
transfers. Proving tests: Hypothesis (random move sequences, including going negative → stock-ledger value = GL
inventory), the rule 27 example, item-transfer value equality.

Two pull requests, as before: **M1.6a** (this plan, model, costing, posting, API, tests) and **M1.6b** (screens).

## Costing (pure, `app/modules/inventory/costing.py`)

The state of a variant is `(qty, value, avg_cost, last_purchase_cost)`; values are in fils, unit costs to 6 places.

- **Inbound** (`q > 0` at unit cost `c`): when `qty ≥ 0`, `value += q·c`, `avg = value / qty`.
- **Inbound into negative stock (rule 27):** the `n = min(q, −qty)` units that were issued below zero are revalued
  at `c`: adjustment `n·(c − u)`, where `u` is the cost they left at (`value / qty`), rounded to fils. A positive
  adjustment is Dr `stock_cost_adjustment` / Cr `inventory` (a `negative_revaluation` move), a negative one the
  other way. Rule 27's example: −10 pcs worth −600, 20 arrive at 65 → adjustment 50; 10 pcs worth 650.
- **Outbound** (`q < 0`): at the current average; with no stock, at the last average, else the last purchase cost,
  else zero (with a warning). Issuing the whole quantity takes the whole value, so no fils are left behind.
- **Transfers** between warehouses leave and enter at the current average: the average and the value don't change.
- **Item Transfer:** out of item A at A's average; into item B with exactly the same total value (B's average then
  updates as for any receipt, including rule 27).

## Recost (rule 28)

A move dated before the variant's latest move (inside an open period) is a backdated move: after posting it, the
variant's later moves are replayed in date order. Every later issue whose cost changes gets a `recost` move with the
value difference, and a journal entry between `inventory` and the issue's `counter_account_id` (Dr/Cr COGS for
sales, stock adjustment for adjustments). Nothing posted is changed. A backdated move into a locked period is
refused like any posting (the period lock of M1.5).

## Documents (ADR 0009)

| doc_type | Lines | Stock | Journal (rule) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `stock_adjustment` | variant, quantity in a unit, `in` (with a unit cost, default the current average) or `out`, warehouse, batch | `adjustment_in` / `adjustment_out` | in: Dr `inventory` / Cr `stock_adjustment` (23); out: Dr `stock_adjustment` / Cr `inventory` (24) |
| `stock_transfer` | variant, quantity; header from / to warehouse | `transfer_out` + `transfer_in` at the current average | none (25) |
| `item_transfer` | `out` lines (source items) and `in` lines (target items); one warehouse | `item_transfer_out` / `item_transfer_in`; the in lines share the out value (see below) | none: one inventory account in the MVP (26) |

Item Transfer value: the total value leaving the out lines enters the in lines. With one in line it takes all of it;
with several, in proportion to their base quantity, the last line taking the rounding remainder — so in = out to
the fils, always.

Drafts, save-and-post, gap-free numbers per branch (`SA-…`, `ST-…`, `IT-…`), period check and lock override exactly
as vouchers (M1.5). Only `stock` products move stock; services and non-stock products are refused.

**Negative stock:** allowed by default (`business.negative_stock_allowed`); posting returns a warning per line that
goes below zero (`inventory.negative_stock_warning`). With `negative_stock_allowed = false` it is refused.

**Batches:** a line of a batch-tracked product names its batch (an `in` line may create it by number, with an
expiry date when the product tracks expiry); `out` lines must name an existing batch. `GET /stock/batches` lists a
variant's batches in a warehouse earliest expiry first (FEFO suggestion). The cost stays one per variant.

## Services other modules call

`inventory.service.post_moves(...)` — receipts and issues from purchasing (M1.7) and sales (M1.8): applies costing,
writes moves, updates caches, returns the cost of each line so the caller can post COGS / GRNI. `inventory.service.
stock_of(...)` for availability. Accounting gets `accounting.service.post_entry(...)` (journal lines by role or
account), used by inventory and later by sales and purchasing.

## API

Permissions (exist since M0.3): `stock_adjustment.*`, `stock_transfer.*`, `item_transfer.*` (branch-scoped);
`product.view` to see stock; `report.view` for valuation and negative stock.

- `GET/POST /stock-documents`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /stock-documents/{id}` (drafts), `POST /stock-documents/{id}/post`
  (`?post=true` on create)
- `GET /stock/balances?business_id=&warehouse_id=&variant_id=&q=` (quantity and value per warehouse, total per item)
- `GET /stock/variants/{id}/ledger?business_id=&warehouse_id=&from=&to=` (moves with running quantity and value)
- `GET /stock/negative?business_id=` (negative-stock report)
- `GET /stock/valuation?business_id=` (stock value vs the inventory account — must be equal)
- `GET /stock/batches?business_id=&variant_id=&warehouse_id=`

## Tests

- **Costing (pure):** inbound averages, outbound at average, whole-quantity issue takes the whole value, the rule 27
  example and its mirror (incoming cheaper), partial cover of negative stock, issue with no stock (last average,
  last purchase cost, zero + warning).
- **Property (Hypothesis, through the database):** random sequences of adjustments in and out (going negative),
  transfers and item transfers → Σ `stock_move.value` = GL inventory balance; cached balances = a rebuild from
  `stock_move`; item transfers: in value = out value.
- **DB:** `stock_move` insert-only (app role and owner); posted document lines immutable; isolation factories for
  every new table.
- **API:** each document type posts its rule; units convert at entry (carton = 24 pcs); service products refused;
  negative-stock warning and the block setting; batch rules and FEFO order; backdated adjustment recosts a later
  issue; numbering; permissions per type and branch; locked period.
- **Screens (M1.6b):** stock balance list, item stock ledger, adjustment / transfer / Item Transfer entry, negative
  stock report, valuation check; English and Arabic.

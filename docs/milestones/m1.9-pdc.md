# M1.9 — PDC register (plan)

Goal: post-dated cheques the way UAE traders actually use them — a customer hands over a stack of cheques dated
over the coming months, a supplier is paid the same way — kept in a register as a **memo until cleared** (A2).
Nothing reaches the ledger until the bank clears the cheque; then the receipt (or payment) is posted on the
clearance date and settles the invoices the cheque was meant for, bill-wise.

Roadmap acceptance: no ledger entry until clearance; a bounce changes only the status; clearance creates the receipt
or payment and settles the intended invoices; statements show outstanding before and after PDCs in hand. Proving
tests: state-machine tests including illegal transitions; the ledger untouched before clearance.

Two pull requests: **M1.9a** — this plan, the register and its API; **M1.9b** — screens (register with a maturity
calendar, the cheque form, clear / bounce / deposit actions, "after PDCs" on statements and ageing).

## The register (posting-rules.md rules 15 to 22)

`pdc` (tenant table, RLS, audited): business, branch, `direction` (`received`, `issued`), party, `cheque_no`,
`bank_name` (the drawer's bank), `cheque_date` (maturity), `amount`, currency, `status`, `deposit_account_id` (our
bank account: where a received cheque is deposited, or which account an issued one is drawn on), `deposited_on`,
`cleared_on`, `bounced_on`, `bounce_reason`, `voucher_id` (the receipt / payment its clearance posted),
`charge_voucher_id` (a bounce charge), `notes`. `pdc_intended_bill`: the open bills the cheque is meant for, with
the amount for each (up to the cheque's amount).

| Event | Direction | From → to | Ledger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15. Received | received | (new) → `in_hand` | none |
| 16. Deposited | received | `in_hand` → `deposited` (bank account, date) | none |
| 17. Cleared | received | `in_hand` or `deposited` → `cleared` (date) | a **receipt voucher** dated the clearance date: Dr bank / Cr customer, allocated against the intended bills (as far as they are still open), the rest an advance |
| 18. Bounced | received | `in_hand` or `deposited` → `bounced` (date, reason) | none; a bank charge, if entered, is an **expense voucher** Dr `bank_charges` / Cr bank |
| 19. Returned | received | `in_hand` → `returned` | none |
| 20. Issued | issued | (new) → `issued` (drawn on a bank account) | none |
| 21. Cleared | issued | `issued` → `cleared` (date) | a **payment voucher**: Dr supplier / Cr bank, allocated against the intended bills, the rest an advance |
| 22. Bounced / cancelled | issued | `issued` → `bounced` or `cancelled` | none |

Every other transition is refused (`invalid_pdc_transition`); `cleared`, `bounced`, `returned` and `cancelled`
are final. A cleared cheque that the bank later returns is handled on its receipt voucher (Cancel or Reverse,
M1.7b), not in the register. Details (cheque number, bank, date, amount, intended bills) can change only while the
cheque is `in_hand` or `issued`. The vouchers are posted through the accounting service (one door), so the period
lock, numbering, bill-wise allocation and audit all apply as for a hand-made voucher.

## Figures

- **Credit control** (M1.4): `pdc_in_hand` = received cheques of the customer still `in_hand` or `deposited`; shown
  beside the ledger balance, never subtracted (A2).
- **Party statement**: `pdc_in_hand` and `outstanding_after_pdc` next to the closing balance.
- **Ageing** (M1.8b): a `pdc_in_hand` column and `after_pdc` per party (receivables: received cheques; payables:
  issued cheques not yet cleared).

## API

Permissions exist (M0.3): `pdc.view`, `pdc.create` (record), `pdc.edit` (change details, deposit, return,
cancel, bounce), `pdc.post` (clear: posts the voucher; also needed for a bounce with a bank charge).

- `GET /pdcs?business_id&direction&status&party_id&due_from&due_to` — the register, by maturity (the calendar)
- `POST /pdcs`, `GET /pdcs/{id}`, `PUT /pdcs/{id}` (details, while not deposited)
- `POST /pdcs/{id}/deposit` `{bank_account_id, date}`, `/clear` `{date, bank_account_id?}`, `/bounce`
  `{date, reason, charge?}`, `/return`, `/cancel`
- Every write takes an `Idempotency-Key`, as all writes do.

## Tests

- **State machine (pure):** every allowed transition per direction, every other one refused.
- **API:** received → deposited → cleared posts one receipt on the clearance date, settles the intended invoices
  (and the rest is an advance); bounced leaves the ledger untouched, with a charge posts only the expense voucher;
  issued → cleared posts the payment; nothing in the ledger before clearance (trial balance and party ledger
  unchanged); details locked after deposit; credit figures and the statement show PDCs in hand; ageing columns;
  permissions (the Cashier records, the Accountant clears); tenant isolation for both new tables.

# M1.3 — Pricing engine (plan)

Goal: when a salesperson picks an item, the system proposes the right price and says where it came from, and
warns when a price is below the item's minimum.

Roadmap acceptance: the engine returns price + `price_source`; the customer history panel shows the customer's own
history only. Proving tests: table-driven tests for each priority level, including a unit-converted last price;
history query tests; the minimum-price warning.

## Order (decision A9) — first match wins, promotions do not stack

1. **Last price sold to this customer** for this variant, in the same unit; if the last sale was in another unit,
   converted by the product's unit factors and flagged `converted`.
2. **Customer's price list**.
3. **Customer group's price list**.
4. **Quantity break** on the business's standard price list (highest `min_qty` ≤ quantity).
5. **Promotion** (active on the date, for the variant, product, category or brand; percent off, amount off or a fixed
   price, applied to the base price).
6. **Base price** — `product_unit.sale_price`.

Then the **minimum-price guard**: a price below `product_unit.min_price` is flagged; posting it later needs
`price.override_min`. The result is stored on the document line as `price_source`, e.g.
`last_price:INV-2026-00412`, `price_list:Wholesale`, `promotion:Ramadan`, `base`.

## What depends on later milestones (built as plug-in points now)

| Step | Needs | Arrives in | M1.3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 last price, history panel | posted sales invoices | M1.8 Sales | Engine step and unit conversion built; a `LastSaleSource` the sales module provides; until then none is found |
| 2–3 customer / group list | customers and customer groups | M1.4 Parties | Engine steps built; the caller passes the customer's and group's price list (M1.4 assigns them to parties) |

The engine is a pure function over these inputs, so every step is tested now with stand-in sources; M1.4 and
M1.8 only connect real data.

## Model (tenant tables: forced RLS, audit, sync, composite tenant keys)

| Table | Columns | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `product_unit` (+) | `sale_price`, `min_price` NUMERIC(19,4), nullable | Base price and minimum per unit; VAT-exclusive (VAT modes arrive with tax, M1.10) |
| `price_list` | `business_id`, `name_en/_ar`, `currency` (AED), `is_standard`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `archived_at` | One standard list per business (step 4) |
| `price_list_item` | `price_list_id`, `variant_id`, `unit_id`, `min_qty` NUMERIC(19,6) ≥ 0, `price` NUMERIC(19,4) ≥ 0 | Unique per list, variant, unit and `min_qty`; the unit must be one of the product's units |
| `promotion` | `business_id`, `name_en/_ar`, `kind` (`percent, amount, fixed_price`), `value` NUMERIC(19,4), `scope` (`variant, product, category, brand`), `scope_id`, `min_qty`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `archived_at` | Percent 0–100; newest active promotion wins when several match. Buy-x-get-y needs whole documents: with sales (M1.8) |

Money is `Decimal`, NUMERIC(19,4); a price converted between units is rounded to 4 decimal places (money
precision), never the quantity (L3).

## API

- `PATCH /products/{id}/units/{unit}/prices` — `sale_price`, `min_price` (`price_list.edit`)
- `GET/POST /price-lists`, `PATCH /price-lists/{id}`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /price-lists/{id}/items` (`price_list.*`)
- `GET/POST /promotions`, `PATCH /promotions/{id}` (new permission `promotion.*`; every role that sees price lists
  sees promotions)
- `POST /pricing/quote` — `{business_id, variant_id, unit, qty, date?, customer_price_list_id?,
  group_price_list_id?}` → `{price, price_source, source_name, converted, min_price, below_min,
  may_go_below_min}` (`price_list.view`)

## Tests

- Table-driven engine tests: each of the six levels wins when the levels above are empty; first match wins;
  promotions never stack; last price in another unit converted and flagged; quantity breaks pick the highest
  `min_qty` ≤ qty; expired lists and promotions ignored; minimum-price warning, and whether the user may go below.
- API: price lists, items (unit must belong to the product), promotions, product unit prices, quote end to end,
  permissions (a salesperson may quote but not edit prices), isolation factories for the new tables.
- Screens: product page gets price and minimum per unit; Settings → Pricing for price lists and promotions;
  English and Arabic.

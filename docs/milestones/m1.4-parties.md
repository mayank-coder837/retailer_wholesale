# M1.4 — Parties and credit control (plan)

Goal: one list of the people and companies a trader deals with — customers, suppliers, or both (ADR 0005) — with
their addresses, contacts and TRN, per-business terms (price list, credit limit, credit days, warn or block), and
a credit check that says whether a sale may go ahead.

Roadmap acceptance: an over-limit order warns or blocks per policy; the override is audited. Proving tests: credit
check (limit, overdue days, override, PDC not reducing the balance).

## Credit check (pure function, `app/modules/parties/credit.py`)

Inputs: the customer's ledger balance, open (unposted) orders, this document's amount, the oldest overdue bill's
age, credit limit, credit days, policy, PDCs in hand, and whether the user holds `credit.override` for the
business. Rules (domain model, decision A2):

- over the limit when balance + open orders + this document > limit (no limit = never over);
- overdue when any bill is older than the credit days;
- either → `warn` or `block` per the policy; `block` can be overridden only with `credit.override`;
- PDCs in hand are reported beside the balance and **never reduce it**.

Overriding writes an audit entry with the new action `override` (`changes.override = "credit"`; who, which party
and business, the document, the figures, the reason) through `record_credit_override`, which sales (M1.8) calls
when an over-limit document is posted.

## Pricing (closes M1.3's plug-in point)

`POST /pricing/quote` takes `party_id`: step 2 uses the customer profile's price list, step 3 the customer group's
list. The temporary `customer_price_list_id` / `group_price_list_id` fields go.

## API

Permissions: `customer.*` on customer profiles and customer parties, `supplier.*` on supplier profiles; a party
that is both needs the role's permission for each side; `credit.limit` (new; Accountant and Owner) to change a
customer's limit, days or policy; `credit.override` to pass a block.

- `GET /parties?q=&role=customer|supplier`, `POST /parties`, `GET/PATCH /parties/{id}`
- `PUT/DELETE /parties/{id}/addresses/{address_id}`, `POST /parties/{id}/addresses`; same for contacts
- `PUT /parties/{id}/customer-profile` and `/supplier-profile` (per business)
- `GET/POST /customer-groups`, `PATCH /customer-groups/{id}`
- `POST /parties/{id}/credit-check` `{business_id, amount}` → decision, reasons, figures, `may_override`

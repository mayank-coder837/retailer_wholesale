# M1.1 — Units of measure engine (plan)

Goal: every quantity in the product keeps its unit, and converting between units is exact and happens once, at
entry (CLAUDE.md rule 6). A trader buys fabric by the roll and sells it by the metre; buys cartons and sells pieces.

Roadmap acceptance: roll/metre and carton/piece conversions exact; lines store entered + base quantity.
Proving tests: Hypothesis — round-trip conversions lose nothing at 6 dp; factor > 0; rounding to unit decimals.

## Model

| Table | Columns | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `unit_family` | `code` (`count, length, weight, volume, area, time`), `name_en/_ar`, `base_unit_id` | One per family per tenant |
| `unit` | `family_id`, `code`, `name_en/_ar`, `symbol`, `decimals` (0–6), `factor_to_family_base` NUMERIC(19,6) > 0, `is_standard`, `archived_at` | `code` unique per tenant. Composite FK `(tenant_id, family_id)` |

Both tables are tenant tables with forced row-level security, audit and sync capture like every other table.

**Standard units per tenant** (ADR 0013). The domain model sketched shared system rows (`tenant_id` null). Instead,
every tenant gets its own copy of the standard units at sign-up (and existing tenants in the migration): rule 1
stays uniform (one RLS policy, no "or null" exception), tenants can rename units and add Arabic names their
customers use, and sync treats units like any other master data.

Standard set: count — `pcs` (piece), `dozen` (12), `pair` (2); length — `m`, `cm` (0.01), `mm` (0.001), `km`
(1000), `in` (0.0254), `ft` (0.3048), `yd` (0.9144); weight — `kg`, `g` (0.001), `ton` (1000), `lb` (0.453592);
volume — `l`, `ml` (0.001), `gal` (3.785412, US); area — `m2`, `ft2` (0.092903); time — `hr`, `day` (24).
Packaging units that vary by product (`carton`, `box`, `roll`, `bag`, `than`) are count units with factor 1 to
`pcs` at family level; their real size is set per product in M1.2 (`product_unit.factor_to_base`: 1 carton = 24
pcs, 1 roll = 50 m).

Simplification against `domain-model.md`: no separate `unit_conversion` table. Each unit stores one factor to its
family's base unit, and any two units of a family convert through it. One factor per unit can never contradict
another the way a table of pairs can.

## Engine (`app/modules/catalog/uom.py`, pure functions, no database)

- `standard_factor(from_unit, to_unit)` → factor within a family; raises `UnitsNotConvertible` across families.
- `to_base(qty_entered, entered_decimals, factor_to_base)` → `LineQuantity(qty_entered, uom_entered,
  factor_to_base, qty_base)`; validates `qty_entered` has at most the entered unit's decimals and `factor_to_base > 0`.
  **Exact, never rounded** (founder, 15 Oct 2026): `qty_base = qty_entered × factor_to_base` with every digit (1 yd =
  0.9144 m); a result that needs more than 6 decimal places is refused (`uom_inexact`) rather than rounded.
- `from_base(qty_base, factor_to_base)` for showing stock in another unit, exactly or refused.
- Product units (M1.2) plug in through `factor_to_base` — the engine does not care where a factor comes from.

## API (permissions `unit.view/create/edit/delete/export`)

- `GET /units` — the tenant's units with family, base unit and factor (archived on request).
- `POST /units` — a custom unit in a family (code, names en/ar, symbol, decimals, factor to family base).
- `PATCH /units/{id}` — names, symbol, decimals; factor and family are fixed once created (documents will store
  converted quantities, and a changed factor would make old lines read differently); archive/unarchive.
- `GET /units/convert?qty=&from=&to=` — standard conversion within a family, for screens.
- Industry packs: the `units` section applier (registered in M0.4, deferred to M1.1) adds the pack's units by code,
  idempotently; an existing code is left as the tenant set it.

Default roles: Owner and Manager everything; Salesperson, Warehouse, Accountant and Cashier get `unit.view`.

## Tests

- Hypothesis: `qty_base` always equals `qty_entered × factor` exactly or the conversion is refused; `to_base`
  then `from_base` always returns what was typed; factor ≤ 0 is rejected; standard factors compose
  (a→b × b→c = a→c within 6 dp).
- Table tests: 1 roll = 50 m, 2.5 rolls = 125 m; 3 cartons × 24 = 72 pcs; 1 yd = 0.9144 m exactly; 0.001 yd and
  metres → yards refused as inexact; 1.5 pcs rejected (pcs has 0 decimals).
- API: list/create/edit/archive, factor/family immutable, duplicate code, validation, permissions (a Salesperson
  can view but not create), cross-tenant isolation for both tables, standard units created at sign-up, pack
  units applied once.
- Migration: existing tenants get the standard units.

## Not in M1.1

the catalog screens in M1.2).

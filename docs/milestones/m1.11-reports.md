# M1.11 — Reports (plan)

Goal: the reports a UAE trader asks their accountant for, read only from the ledgers (rule 5: stock from stock
moves; money from posted journal lines), in English and Arabic, under one **Reports** menu.

Roadmap acceptance: reports read only from ledgers; the trial balance balances. Proving tests: report tests against
hand-computed fixtures; the balance sheet balances whenever the trial balance does.

Already built (linked from Reports, not rebuilt): trial balance (M1.5), receivables / payables ageing with the PDC
column (M1.8, M1.9), party ledger / statement (M1.5), stock balances, stock valuation and negative stock (M1.6), the
VAT return draft (M1.10).

## M1.11a — statements (API)

New module `app/modules/reporting` (as `CLAUDE.md` lists): pure builders in `statements.py` (no database), a service
that reads through `accounting.service` only (one door: the module never queries accounting's tables), a router.
All three need `report.view` for the business.

- **`GET /reports/profit-loss?business_id&from&to`** — each income and expense account's movement in the period,
  in the classic trading layout:
  - **Sales** (`sales` accounts, credit-positive) less **cost of goods sold** (`cogs`) = **gross profit**;
  - plus **other income** (`other_income`) less **expenses** (`expense`) = **net profit** (negative: a loss).
  - Rows per account (code, names, amount), a subtotal per section; accounts with no movement are left out.
- **`GET /reports/balance-sheet?business_id&as_of`** — each balance-sheet account's balance on the date:
  - **Assets**: current (cash, bank, receivables, stock, VAT input, other current) and fixed;
  - **Liabilities**: current (payables, VAT output, other current) and non-current;
  - **Equity**: equity accounts, plus the profit not yet closed into equity (there is no year-end closing yet):
    **profit for the year** (from the start of the fiscal year containing the date) and **earlier years' profit**
    (before it) — open question **R10**.
  - Assets = liabilities + equity, always, because the journal balances; a `balanced` flag says so (it is checked,
    not assumed). Tax accounts sit by nature (VAT input asset, VAT output liability).
- **`GET /reports/day-book?business_id&from&to`** — every posted journal entry in the period, oldest first: date,
  number, what it came from (document type, id), narration, its lines (account, party, debit, credit). As on every
  ledger, a cancelled document and its mirror are left out (Cancel means it never happened); a reversal appears on
  its own date. At most 500 entries per call, with `truncated` when there are more (narrow the dates).
- Amounts are exact decimals (`NUMERIC`), signed as accountants read them; the screen rounds for display only.
- Tests: pure builder tests; API tests against a hand-computed fixture (a small trading month: capital, a purchase,
  sales on credit, an expense, a receipt) checking every figure; balance sheet balanced and equal to the trial
  balance's totals; a property test (random balanced journals → the balance sheet balances and net profit equals
  income − expenses); permissions (403 without `report.view`) and a cross-business check.

## M1.11b — screens

- **Reports** in the main menu (today "coming soon"): a page grouping every report — Accounts (P&L, balance sheet,
  trial balance, day book), Customers and suppliers (ageing, statements), Stock (balances, valuation, negative stock),
  VAT (VAT return).
- P&L page: period picker (this month, last month, this fiscal year, dates); sections with subtotals; gross and net
  profit lines; an account opens its ledger.
- Balance sheet page: date picker; the two sides; the "not yet closed" profit lines explained; an account opens its
  ledger.
- Day book page: period picker; entries with their lines; a number opens its document.
- English and Arabic (RTL), screen tests, an e2e check that the P&L's net profit and the balance sheet agree.

## Open questions

- **R10** — Until year-end closing exists, the balance sheet shows the profit not yet transferred to equity as two
  lines: profit for the fiscal year to date, and earlier years' profit. Should earlier years' profit be called
  "retained earnings", and when closing comes, should it post a closing journal or stay a report-only roll-forward?

## Built — M1.11a (statements API)

- Module `app/modules/reporting`: `statements.py` (pure: `profit_loss`, `balance_sheet`, `profit`), `service.py`
  (reads through `accounting.service` and `parties.service` only), `router.py`, `schemas.py`.
- `accounting.service` gained `chart_accounts`, `fiscal_year_start` (the fiscal year containing a date, or its bounds
  from the business's start month) and `day_book` (the shared ledger query over all accounts); ledger lines carry
  their `entry_id`.
- P&L movement = balance at the end − balance the day before the start (the trial balance's figures, no new query).
- Endpoints `GET /reports/profit-loss`, `/reports/balance-sheet`, `/reports/day-book` (`report.view`, 422
  `invalid_report_period` when the period ends before it starts).
- Tests: `test_reports_api.py` (a hand-computed trading month: every P&L, balance-sheet and day-book figure; the
  trial balance agrees; 403 for a cashier; bad period) and `test_reporting_statements.py` (layout, signs, profit
  split, and a Hypothesis property: any balanced journal gives a balanced sheet and a profit split that adds up —
  probed once with a broken split).

# M1.7 — Purchasing (plan)

Goal: the buying side of a trader's day — purchase orders, goods received (in part or in full), the supplier's bill
(matched to what was received, any price difference to the variance account), returns to the supplier, and Cancel /
Reverse for every posted purchase document.

Roadmap acceptance: the three-step flow posts per rules 7 to 9; GRNI clears to zero; Cancel removes the effects dated
on the original date and frees the number; Reverse creates a dated debit note. Proving tests: posting tests per rule;
partial receipt tests; cancel / reverse tests (ledger, stock, VAT, payables, number reuse, audit snapshot).

Three pull requests:

- **M1.7a** — this plan, the four purchase documents, their posting, and the API.
- **M1.7b** — Cancel and Reverse, built once for every posted document type: purchase documents, the vouchers of M1.5
  and the stock documents of M1.6 (sales use it in M1.8).
- **M1.7c** — screens.

## Documents (ADR 0009: `document` + `document_line`)

| doc_type | Lines | Posts (rule) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `purchase_order` | item, quantity in a unit, price per unit, VAT rate | nothing: an order. Posting confirms it and gives it its number (`PO-…`) |
| `goods_receipt` | item, quantity received, the order line it fulfils (optional), price per unit (default the order's) | stock in at the line's value; Dr `inventory` / Cr `grni` (7) |
| `purchase_bill` | the receipt line it bills (or none: a direct bill), item or expense account, quantity, price, VAT rate | receipt lines: Dr `grni` (the value received for the billed quantity), Dr / Cr `purchase_price_variance` (the difference), Dr `vat_input` / Cr `ap_control` (8). Direct stock lines: Dr `inventory` instead of `grni` (and stock in). Expense / service lines: Dr the expense account |
| `purchase_return` | the bill line or receipt line it returns, quantity | stock out at the current average. Against a bill: Dr `ap_control` (price + VAT) / Cr `inventory` (average), Cr `vat_input`, difference to `stock_cost_adjustment` (9). Against an unbilled receipt: Dr `grni` / Cr `inventory`, difference to `stock_cost_adjustment` |

- **Partial receipts:** an order line's received quantity is the sum of the posted receipt lines that fulfil it
  (never a stored counter); a receipt may not take more than is still open on its order line. An order is `open`,
  `partly received` or `received` by that sum.
- **Matching (A4):** a bill line names the receipt line it bills; it may bill no more than is still unbilled there.
  The GRNI it clears is the receipt line's value × billed quantity / received quantity (the last bill takes what is
  left, so GRNI clears to exactly zero per receipt line). Bill net − GRNI cleared → `purchase_price_variance`.
- **VAT (A3, until tax codes in M1.10):** per line, rate 0 or 5 %, prices VAT-exclusive; VAT per line half-up to
  fils, total VAT = sum of lines. Blocked VAT (SR-B) arrives with tax codes.
- **Payables:** a posted bill creates a payable `bill` (bill-wise, M1.5) for its grand total with the supplier's
  reference, due after the supplier's payment terms. A return against a bill settles that bill first; anything over
  is an advance. Payments (M1.5) settle bills as before.
- **Prices:** `unit_price` is per unit entered (per carton when bought by the carton); values are quantity ×
  price in fils. Stock enters at the line's net value, per base unit to six places.
- Gap-free numbers per branch (`PO-`, `GRN-`, `PB-`, `PR-`), period lock and override, drafts, save-and-post — as
  every document so far. A receipt and a return name one warehouse; a bill with stock lines (direct) does too.

## Model changes (migration 0011)

`document`: `due_date`, `supplier_ref` (the supplier's bill number), `subtotal`, `tax_total` (`total` is the grand
total). `document_line`: `unit_price` (per unit entered, 6 places), `net_amount`, `vat_rate`, `vat_amount`,
`line_total`, `account_id` (expense lines), `source_line_id` (the order / receipt / bill line it follows),
`cleared_value` (GRNI cleared by a bill line; the cost returned by a return line — set at post).

## Cancel and Reverse (M1.7b, A7; posting-rules.md rules 32 and 33) — built

One service, `documents/service.py` (`cancel`, `reverse`), for every posted type; endpoints
`POST /documents/{id}/cancel` and `/reverse` (`<type>.cancel` / `<type>.reverse`, a reason; reverse takes a date and the
period-lock override like posting). Nothing posted is changed: every effect comes back out by new rows (migration 0012).

- **Books.** Journal entries now have a `kind`: `posting`, `recost` (rule 28) or `undo`. Undo mirrors only the
  document's `posting` entries (debit ↔ credit, `reversal_of_id` → the entry; one mirror per entry). Its recosts of
  *other* moves are not mirrored: the stock replay below corrects them.
- **Bill-wise.** Allocations get negative copies (`bill_allocation.reversal_of_id`), never edits. What the document's
  lines settled is open again; payments others allocated to its bills become **advances** on those payments; then
  the mirror lines close its bills.
- **Stock.** Each quantity move gets one `cancel` / `reversal` row (`stock_move.reversal_of_id`, once per move):
  - *Cancel* (dated on the original date) takes back the move's whole value (own, recosts, revaluations). Replay
    skips the pair, so later moves are recosted **as if the document never existed** (journals dated on the moves
    they correct; refused if one is in a locked period).
  - *Reverse* (dated on the chosen day) acts where it stands: goods that left come back at what they left at
    (rule 27 below zero); goods that came in leave at the **current average** and the difference to what they came
    in at goes to stock cost adjustment (open question R5); a transfer takes back exactly what it moved.
  - The stock value the mirror does not cover (recosts since, rule 27, that difference) is posted in one more
    `undo` entry, so stock value = the inventory account always.
- **Cancel**: only in an open period (else "reverse it instead"); orders and other commitments can be cancelled any
  time and never reversed. The number is released; `GET /documents/released-numbers` lists freed numbers and
  posting with `reuse_number` takes one (the new document's `reissue_of_id` points at the cancelled one). Status
  `cancelled`; the audit entry holds the full snapshot (header, lines, entry).
- **Reverse**: a reversal document — a debit note for a purchase bill (a credit note for a sales invoice, M1.8),
  otherwise one of the same type — numbered in its own series, holding the mirror entries and reversal rows; status
  `reversed`; both stay visible in ledgers. The reversal carries the header only (no lines yet).
- A document others follow (an order received, a receipt billed or returned) is refused until they are undone; a
  reversal cannot itself be cancelled or reversed.
- Ledgers hide a cancelled document's entry and its mirror (they net to nothing), so it shows once.
- Not yet: the "VAT return filed" and "sent as an e-invoice" guards (no VAT returns until M1.10, no ASP yet); the
  warning before reusing a printed number.

## Screens (M1.7c) — built

- **Purchases** in the sidebar: the list (type and status filters, supplier names), "New order", "Receive goods",
  "Enter a bill". A return has no button there: it starts from the bill or receipt it returns.
- **Entry form** for all four types. `?from=<doc>` starts it from an earlier document (an order to receive, a
  receipt to bill or return, a bill to return): supplier, branch, warehouse and the lines still open, each showing
  what is open. Bills take expense lines and the supplier's bill number and due date (default: their terms).
  Totals as the API computes them (VAT per line, half-up). A number freed by a cancelled document of that type can
  be chosen ("Reuse PB-…"), posted with `reuse_number`.
- **Document page**: header, lines, totals, progress (open / partly received / received / billed); a draft is
  posted or deleted; a posted one shows its next step while lines are still open.
- **Cancel / Reverse panel** (`components/correction-panel.tsx`) on purchase, stock and voucher pages: where the
  document stands (cancelled with reason, reversed by …, reverses …, reissued after …, all linked) from
  `GET /documents/{id}`, and the two actions with a reason, a date (reverse) and the period-lock override.
- VAT: a goods receipt, and a return of goods not yet billed, carry no VAT (nothing is owed yet; the rate waits
  for the bill). The API now records VAT 0 on such returns; their posting never used it.
- Later: lines for batch-tracked items started from an earlier document (the batch is asked for by the API), a
  progress label for a receipt returned in full (it reads "billed"), and number reuse on the voucher and stock
  entry forms.

## API

Permissions exist (M0.3): `purchase_order.*`, `goods_receipt.*`, `purchase_bill.*`, `purchase_return.*`.

- `GET/POST /purchase-documents`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /purchase-documents/{id}`, `POST /purchase-documents/{id}/post`
- `GET /purchase-documents/{id}/open-lines` — what is still to receive (an order) or to bill (a receipt), to start
  the next document from
- M1.7b: `POST /documents/{id}/cancel`, `POST /documents/{id}/reverse` (every document type)
- M1.7c: `GET /documents/{id}` — where any document stands for Cancel / Reverse (its reversal, what it reverses,
  the cancelled document whose number it reuses)

## Tests

- **Posting (pure):** rules 7, 8 (with and without price difference, direct bill, expense line), 9 (against a bill,
  against a receipt; average ≠ price).
- **API:** order → two partial receipts → bill → GRNI zero per receipt line; over-receiving and over-billing refused;
  bill-wise payable with due date; return against a bill reduces it; expense bill; units (bought by the carton);
  permissions; numbering; period lock.
- **Property (Hypothesis, database):** random orders, partial receipts, bills (with price changes) and returns → the
  trial balance balances, GRNI = Σ (received − billed) value, stock value = inventory account, supplier balance = Σ
  open payable bills.
- **M1.7b:** cancel / reverse per type: ledger, stock, payables and VAT back to before; number reuse; audit snapshot;
  dependants block.
